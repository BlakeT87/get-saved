He wants you in His family.

Take three quiet minutes. We’ll walk through the good news of Jesus, one step at a time, and end with a simple prayer.

Step 1 of 5

God made you, and He loves you.

You are not an accident. God created you on purpose, knows you by name, and wants to be close to you, right now, exactly as you are.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only born Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.”John 3:16 (WEB)
Next
Step 2 of 5

Sin has separated us from Him.

Every one of us has done wrong, and has gone our own way instead of God’s. The Bible calls this sin. It builds a wall between us and a holy God, and we can’t take it down ourselves.

“For all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God.”Romans 3:23 (WEB)
Next
Step 3 of 5

Jesus paid the price for you.

God didn’t leave us stuck. Jesus, God’s Son, lived the perfect life we couldn’t. He died on the cross to take the punishment for our sin, and He rose from the dead, defeating death. Forgiveness and new life are a gift, not something you earn.

“But God commends his own love toward us, in that while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”Romans 5:8 (WEB)
“For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”Romans 6:23 (WEB)
Next
Step 4 of 5

You receive this gift by faith.

You don’t need to clean up your life first. You simply turn from going your own way, believe that Jesus died and rose for you, and invite Him to be your Lord and Savior.

“If you will confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord, and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.”Romans 10:9 (WEB)

If that is what you want, you can tell God right now. Prayer is just talking to Him.

Continue to the prayer
Step 5 of 5

Pray this out loud, or in your heart.

These exact words aren’t magic. God looks at your heart. If you mean it, pray along:

Dear God,

I know I’m not perfect, and I’ve sinned. I’m sorry, and I want to turn away from it.

I believe that Jesus is Your Son, that He died on the cross for my sins, and that You raised Him from the dead.

Jesus, I ask You to forgive me. Come into my life. Be my Lord and my Savior.

Thank You for loving me, for saving me, and for making me part of Your family.

In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Welcome

Welcome to God’s family! 🎉

The Bible says heaven celebrates when one person turns to God. What you just did is the most important decision of your life.

Your next steps
  1. Tell someone. Share your decision with a Christian friend or the person who gave you this page.
  2. Read the Bible. Start with the Gospel of John.
  3. Talk to God daily. Prayer is simply a conversation.
  4. Find a church. Grow alongside others who follow Jesus.
  5. Be baptized. It’s a public celebration of your new life.
